## Design Context

### Users
Mac users who dictate throughout their day and need an unobtrusive, glanceable indication that Talkie is listening, processing, or finished without interrupting their current app.

### Brand Personality
Calm, private, and quietly expressive. Talkie should feel native to macOS and confident without looking loud, synthetic, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined organic minimalism: mostly monochrome, transparent waveform forms that float over the desktop. Motion should resemble breath, ink, and fabric in a light current—not an equalizer, oscilloscope, or mechanical meter. The production pill remains available as the practical baseline.

### Design Principles
- Let speech energy shape the motion, but smooth it enough to feel alive rather than reactive.
- Prefer one continuous gesture over many decorative effects.
- Use color only for meaningful success and error feedback.
- Preserve legibility with reduced motion and increased contrast.
- Keep experiments native SwiftUI and isolated from Release builds.
